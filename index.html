Privacy Policy for Hadya-e-Quran

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Hadya-e-Quran helps organizers manage Quran Khwani groups, participant names, Sipara assignments, and recurring rounds. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information the app handles

  • Account information: your email address and, if you provide them, your name and optional phone number.
  • Group information: group names, participant names, Sipara assignments, and round or schedule settings that you enter.
  • Messages to us: the contact details and message content you submit through Contact Us, Report a Bug, or app-rating feedback.

Some information can be kept on your device. If you use an account and cloud syncing, account and group information is processed by Supabase to provide sign-in and syncing between your devices.

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • provide account sign-in and sync your groups across devices;
  • save and display the groups and assignments you create;
  • respond to contact messages and bug reports; and
  • send account-related emails, such as verification messages.

Service providers and sharing

We use Supabase for account authentication and cloud services. We use Resend to deliver account-related and support emails. These providers process information to provide services for the app.

We do not sell your personal information. Hadya-e-Quran does not display advertisements. If you choose to share a snapshot, your device's sharing options let you select another app or recipient.

Snapshots, photos, and notifications

Snapshots are created for you to review and save or share. When you save a snapshot, it is saved to your device's photo gallery. The app may request notification permission for scheduled snapshot reminders or saving. You can manage these permissions in your device settings.

Data retention and deletion requests

Account and synced group information is kept while needed to provide the service. To request deletion of your account and associated information, email us from your registered email address at hadya.e.quran@gmail.com. We may ask you to verify the request before processing it.

Security

We use service providers and safeguards intended to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version will be posted on this page with its effective date.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: hadya.e.quran@gmail.com
